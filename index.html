Significance to the Community

Bridging the gap between expensive personal training and accessible everyday public health.

01

Free Virtual Coaching for All

Personal trainers and commercial gym memberships are costly. By running directly in a web browser on any phone or laptop, this AI system gives students, workers, and low-income households free exercise guidance at home.

02

Injury Prevention & Safer Form

Improper exercise execution leads to joint strain and preventable injuries among beginners. Instant visual recognition encourages disciplined movement patterns and safer solo workouts without needing a spotter.

03

Combating Sedentary Lifestyles

Interactive AI feedback gamifies daily physical activity, helping schools, barangay wellness programs, and busy professionals stay consistent and reduce risks of lifestyle-related health conditions.

04

Privacy-First Edge AI

All video processing runs locally inside the user's browser via TensorFlow.js. No webcam footage is ever recorded or uploaded to external servers, protecting community members' privacy while building confidence.
